#  Jumia E-Commerce Power BI Dashboard

##  Executive Summary
An end-to-end Business Intelligence solution designed in Power BI to analyze e-commerce sales performance, product category margins, customer retention, and regional profitability.

---

##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** $91.51M
- **Total Cost:** $63.96M
- **Net Profit:** $27.55M
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 30.11%
- **YoY Growth:** 92.44%
- **Total Orders:** 50K (across 80 Active Products)
- **Repeat Purchase Rate:** 100% (2K Repeat Customers)

---

##  Key Business Insights
- **Revenue Dominance vs. Margin:** Electronics and Home Appliances generate over 90% of total revenue. However, their per-unit profit margins are relatively lower compared to volume.
- **High-Margin Opportunity:** The **Books** category generates only 2.18% of total revenue but delivers a high profit margin of **27.55%**, presenting a prime cross-selling opportunity.
- **Customer Loyalty:** Strong customer retention with a 100% repeat purchase rate across 2,000 active repeat buyers.
- **Geographic Performance:** Regional profit margins remain tightly consistent across global markets (~29.0% – 29.6%).

---

##  Technical Implementation
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Automated ETL pipeline built using **Power Query**.
- **Data Modeling:** Designed an optimized **Star Schema** data model for high query performance.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Implemented dynamic measures, dynamic visuals, and key metrics using **DAX**.
